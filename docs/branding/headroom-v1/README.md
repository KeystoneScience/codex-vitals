## Logo prompt

Use case: logo-brand.
Create an original, professional logo for "Headroom", a desktop utility showing available AI usage capacity, accounts, and resets. The brand idea is room to keep working: breathing space, possibility, available capacity.
Deliver a single primary horizontal logo lockup on a genuinely transparent background. Wide landscape canvas with comfortable margins, approximately 3:1 aspect ratio. Symbol on the left, wordmark on the right, optically aligned.
Design the symbol as a memorable, compact abstract lowercase h / open architectural space. Use confident, substantial softly squared geometric strokes, a low connecting beam, and a lifted top element that creates an intentional open gap: the negative space above the low beam expresses "headroom". The silhouette must remain simple and recognizable at 24 pixels. Aim for a distinctive modern software identity, not a literal house. Two or three geometric elements at most. Be optically balanced and beautifully proportioned.
Wordmark text, exactly: "headroom". Set in a carefully kerned contemporary humanist sans serif, medium/semibold, lower case, relaxed but precise. Slightly rounded terminals and generous open counters. No tagline.
Palette: symbol in saturated fresh mint-teal (approximately #31BEA1, dark enough on white), wordmark in deep charcoal #152724.
Style: flat, precise, clean, vector-like edges. Modern independent Mac software, understated and timeless. Lots of breathing room.
Avoid: heartbeat lines, medical symbols, circular gauges, batteries, charts, arrows, clouds, brains, robot heads, sparkles, infinity knots, roofs, 3D, gradients, glow, shadows, mockups, extra text, explanatory labels, watermarks, extra variations. One unified logo only. Actual transparent alpha outside the logo; no checkerboard drawn in the image.
